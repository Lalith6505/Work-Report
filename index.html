Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Developer: Lalith Madanayake

Work Done Report Generator is built with strict privacy and data security. The app operates 100% locally on your device.

Data Collection & Processing

We do not collect, transmit, track, or share any personal data, client information, photographs, or signatures. All report details, measurements, photos, and signatures remain strictly within your device's private local storage.

Permissions

  • Camera (Optional): Used only when taking on-site photos directly inside the app.
  • Photo Picker: Uses Android's Zero-Permission Photo Picker so the app only accesses images you explicitly select.
  • Storage: Saves generated PDFs to your public Downloads folder via MediaStore. No broad external storage access is required.

Sharing

When you choose to share reports via WhatsApp, Email, or cloud storage, documents are passed securely through Android's system Sharesheet under your direct control.
