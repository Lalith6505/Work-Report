Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026

App Name: Work Done Report Generator (Package: com.workdone.reportgenerator)

Developer: Lalith Madanayake

Key Summary: Work Done Report Generator does NOT collect, transmit, track, or share any personal data, client information, photographs, or signatures. All report creation, image processing, and PDF compilation take place 100% locally on your device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Lalith Madanayake ("we", "us", or "our", in association with engineering standards of Sisaara Engineers (Pvt) Ltd) treats information when you use the Work Done Report Generator mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining the highest security and confidentiality standards for your corporate, engineering, and personal data.

2. Data Collection and Usage

The application functions as a local productivity and document generation tool:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide any credentials to use the app.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application does not contain third-party analytics trackers, telemetry software, or behavioral tracking SDKs.
  • No Advertising: The application does not display advertisements or transmit advertising identifiers (GAID).
  • No Server Uploads: Information entered into job cards (vessel names, locations, descriptions, dates, engineer names) is stored locally within the application's private storage on your device.

3. Device Permissions & Rationale

Work Done Report Generator is built to strictly adhere to Google Play Developer Policies by avoiding dangerous or unnecessary permissions:

  • Camera (Optional): Used solely when you choose to capture real-time on-site photographs directly inside a job card. Photos are saved directly into the application's local cache and are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Photos & Media (Zero Permission Photo Picker): To select Before and After photos from your device, the app utilizes the native Android Photo Picker. This provides the app with access only to the specific photos you select, without requesting broad read access to your device's photo library.
  • Storage & File Saving: Generated PDF reports are saved to your public Downloads/WorkDoneReports directory via the standard Android MediaStore API or shared via Android's native Sharesheet. No broad external storage access is required or requested on Android 10 and above.

4. On-Device Digital Signatures

When you capture a technician or client signature via the in-app touch pad, the resulting image is rendered and stored locally on your device solely to be embedded into the final PDF document. It is never uploaded to any remote server.

5. Sharing & Exporting

Any PDF reports you share via WhatsApp, Email, Telegram, Google Drive, or other communication channels are dispatched strictly through Android's secure FileProvider mechanism at your explicit direction. We have no access to the destination or recipient of files you choose to share.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all draft reports, custom logos, and images are stored solely within your device's internal application storage:

  • You can delete individual reports at any time inside the app's Saved Reports screen.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing its application data in your Android system settings permanently removes all locally stored drafts and temporary images.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is designed for engineering, field service, and contractor professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
